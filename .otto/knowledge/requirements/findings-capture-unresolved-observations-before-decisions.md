# Findings capture unresolved observations before decisions

<!-- compiled_truth -->

A Knowledge finding captures something noticed or discovered that may matter later, before there is a reason to act on it or an understanding of its cause. Findings are collected and retrieved alongside other Knowledge records when relevant work begins. They do not imply an architectural decision, a remediation plan, or resolved analysis. In the normal new-Knowledge type picker, Finding is the first option.
