# Explicit chat archive and delete choices

<!-- compiled_truth -->

Chat management should expose distinct Archive and Delete actions in tab menus. Closing a chat tab should offer Cancel, Delete, and Archive; Delete requires a final irreversible-delete confirmation, and warning suppression may only auto-archive a non-running chat.
