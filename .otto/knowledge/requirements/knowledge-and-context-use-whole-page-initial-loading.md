# Knowledge and Context use whole-page initial loading

<!-- compiled_truth -->

Manage Knowledge and Context Management hide their tab content until the initial data load completes. They show one centered page loading state with a label, then reveal the already-populated screen with a short fade only when animations are enabled; they do not show duplicate pane-level loading states or spinners.
