# Project Knowledge is the sole repository home for project records

<!-- compiled_truth -->

Otto Knowledge is the sole repository-owned home for durable project records, including project charters, delivery history, evaluated references, and completed measured investigations. Historical investigations must remain fully discoverable as canonical Knowledge records with their dated evidence and retired hypotheses preserved; they must not require a parallel top-level `findings/` documentation tree.
