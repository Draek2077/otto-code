# Why Android over the relay goes sluggish with Brain and many chats

<!-- compiled_truth -->

Mobile sluggishness with Brain is not a general RPC overload. Chat stream fanout is already gated (mobile subscribes only to the focused chat), inbound validation is already off runtime Zod, and Brain status snapshots are coalesced at 1 Hz busy / 5 s idle. Three specific paths carry the cost instead: (1) `brain_log_line_added` was an unfiltered daemon broadcast of every llama-server log line to every connected socket, delivered to phones that never opened the Logs tab and then dropped client-side; (2) the relay E2EE channel sends all JSON protocol traffic base64-encoded because the binary-framing branch keys off the plaintext being an ArrayBuffer, and Otto protocol messages are strings; (3) the openai-compat provider emits one `agent_stream` WebSocket frame per SSE delta with no daemon-side coalescing. Item (1) is fixed by watch-gating. Items (2) and (3) are open.
