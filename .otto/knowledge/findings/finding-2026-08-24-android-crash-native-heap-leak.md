# Android crashes are three separate causes; the dominant one is a chat-specific native heap leak

<!-- compiled_truth -->

Otto's Android app crashes from three unrelated causes, not one. (1) `getTransformOrigin` returned a CSS keyword string that reached `RCTView`'s `ArrayPropSetter` and threw `ClassCastException`; fixed by returning the resolved `[x, y, z]` array. (2) A transform array entry reaches native with zero keys and throws `ArrayIndexOutOfBoundsException: length=0; index=0` in `TransformHelper.processTransform`; source not located. (3) The dominant cause, three of five recorded crashes, is native-heap exhaustion (`std::bad_alloc`) driven by a foreground-only, render-driven leak that is specific to which chat is open and scales with transcript content. The leak's root component is NOT yet identified; five candidates have been eliminated by measurement.
